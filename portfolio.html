03 Featured project

River Cleaning Boat

A prototype designed to collect floating plastic and waste from river water using a remotely controlled mechanism.

The project combines mechanical design, Bluetooth-based remote control and hands-on prototype testing.

Problem

Floating plastic and waste can accumulate on water surfaces and create environmental problems.

Concept

A compact floating boat mechanism designed to collect floating waste.

Control

Bluetooth-based control from a mobile device.

Prototype

A physical prototype was developed and tested.

Testing

During testing, the collection mechanism was modified from approximately 30° to 45° to improve waste collection performance.

Result

The modified mechanism demonstrated improved collection performance during prototype testing.
